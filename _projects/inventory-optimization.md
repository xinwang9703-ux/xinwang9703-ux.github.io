As a team member, I contribute to early-stage inventory-optimization work that connects product and demand structure with inventory-policy modeling.

The current work includes:

- channel-aware SKU segmentation based on sales volume and demand patterns;
- set segmentation that combines SKU co-sales patterns with business-defined hard-set rules; and
- early-stage set-aware safety-stock and multi-echelon inventory optimization modeling.
