This research develops a predictive opportunistic maintenance policy for a serial-parallel multi-station manufacturing system with heterogeneous components. The study connects equipment-condition information with maintenance decisions in a complex production setting.

The paper was published in _Reliability Engineering & System Safety_ {% cite lu2025maintenance %}.

**Research areas:** predictive maintenance, manufacturing systems, stochastic decision-making
