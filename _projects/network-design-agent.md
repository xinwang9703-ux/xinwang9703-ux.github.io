As the Network Design Team Lead within Ashley Furniture's AI Innovation team, I connect mathematical optimization, enterprise data pipelines, LLM-assisted scenario orchestration, and business decision support in one traceable workflow.

The work currently covers:

- **Optimization model:** a mixed-integer conic programming model that jointly evaluates facility openings, origin-destination and transportation-mode assignments, and MDC-RFC replenishment across cost, working capital, service, and SLA/OTIF objectives.
- **Data pipeline:** reproducible Python, SQL, and PySpark workflows that transform enterprise demand, facility, transportation, and inventory data into validated, versioned model inputs.
- **Agent architecture:** an initial DeerFlow-based workflow linking LLM tool orchestration with Databricks compute and optimization jobs, turning natural-language scenarios into traceable solver runs.
- **Business implementation:** collaboration with business and executive teams to define assumptions and KPIs, explain model trade-offs, and translate validated scenarios into planning recommendations.

**Public scope:** This summary describes the problem and workflow at a high level. It intentionally excludes confidential company data, parameters, and business results.
